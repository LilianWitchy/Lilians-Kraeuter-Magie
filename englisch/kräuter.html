My Herbal World

Nature is full of little treasures. Join me in discovering the fascinating world of herbs, plants and mushrooms, with botanical knowledge, natural recipe ideas and a healthy curiosity about the diversity of the natural world.

Herbs & Natural Healing

Nature offers us countless treasures that can accompany us on our journey towards health and well-being. I have a keen interest in herbs and plants and would like to introduce you to natural ideas that can support your well-being and personal care.

My herbal ideas – from teas and incense to natural skincare applications – are intended to inspire you to nurture your inner balance and discover a deeper connection with nature.

The plants I use in my recipe ideas are more than just ingredients to me. They remind us of how much there is to discover outdoors and how soothing small, mindful rituals can be in everyday life.

My knowledge grows with every plant, every new discovery and every question I have yet to answer. Nature is incredibly diverse, and there is always something new to learn.
