When Words Become Music

Sometimes it begins with words, sometimes with a melody – and sometimes both emerge at the same time.

When inspiration strikes, I begin to write. Thoughts become words, and words become songs.

My songs tell stories of life’s moments: of joy, peace, and hope, but also of the many little and big emotions that accompany us in everyday life.

To me, words are a special kind of magic. They can move thoughts, express feelings, and sometimes even set small inner processes in motion.

That is exactly the power I try to bring to life in my songs.

If you’re curious, simply click on an image. You’ll be taken directly to my YouTube channel, where you can listen to the song.

Perhaps one of my songs will touch you, make you smile, or accompany you for a little while on your journey.
