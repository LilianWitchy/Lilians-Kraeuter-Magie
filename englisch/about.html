With heart, imagination, and a love of nature

Who is behind the magic?

My name is Lilian, and I am Elisabeth-Ann Wolf. This is my pen name, under which you can find all my work. I've been fascinated by nature, its plants, and the many possibilities they offer for a long time. I especially enjoy learning about herbs, their properties, and how they can be used in everyday life.

I am a state-recognised early childhood educator, hold a bachelor's degree, and have trained as an experiential educator. Through many years of working in kindergartens, I've gained a wealth of experience that has shaped my love of stories, imagination, and creative expression.

On my website, a love of nature, creative ideas, and a touch of magic come together. I love trying new things, sharing knowledge, and turning little ideas into something of my own.

My Journey and My Stories

From working with children to fantastical dream journeys and books for adults.

For me, writing is a wonderful way to transform thoughts, imagination, and experiences into stories. That's how my "Dream Journeys with Baldur" came to life – stories for children that invite them to dream and explore.

The first two volumes are already available. More ideas and projects are waiting to take shape. Alongside children's stories, I also write books for adults, continually finding inspiration in my interests and experiences.

I enjoy projects that allow me to learn, experiment, and bring my different interests together. Things don't always turn out as planned – sometimes, taking a little detour leads to a completely new idea.

What Makes My Heart Glow

I have a wide range of interests. What they all share is the joy of creating things with my own hands, imagination, and a love of detail.

Herbs and Nature

Discovering herbs, learning about plants, putting together herbal and incense blends, creating teas, and mindfully enjoying nature's treasures. Herbal drinks, flower preserves, juices, sweets, and food preservation are also among my interests.

Personal Care and Craftsmanship

I enjoy making my own soaps, ointments, creams, natural cosmetics, and skincare and haircare products. Knitting, crocheting, and creating historical clothing are also among the creative activities I love to spend time on.

Words and Imagination

Writing stories, developing dream journeys, creating books, and immersing myself in fantastical worlds: words can open up new spaces and carry us away from everyday life for a while. This possibility continues to inspire me.

Creativity and Balance

Music, yoga, natural skincare, relaxation, and finding balance are important to me. I'm also interested in web development and turning my own ideas into digital projects. I love combining different skills and learning new things.
